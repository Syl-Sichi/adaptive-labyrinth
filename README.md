# Adaptive Labyrinth

**A living maze for digital monsters.**

The Adaptive Labyrinth is a next-generation defense system. Unlike static firewalls or signature-based antivirus, its walls and paths are constantly moving and reshaping themselves in real time. The sole purpose: force the Minotaur (malware, zero-days, APTs, or any intruder) into the absolute center — the Kill Zone — and destroy it.

In the old myths, Daedalus built a labyrinth to contain a monster.  
We built one to hunt them.

Static walls failed. Signatures became obsolete. Threats learned to move through the cracks between traditional defenses. So the Labyrinth was redesigned — not as a prison, but as a predator. Its corridors are alive. Its walls rearrange themselves with purpose. Every path exists to drive the Minotaur toward the center, where no escape remains and destruction is absolute.

The Adaptive Labyrinth does not wait to be breached.  
It hunts.

---

## Core Concept

- **Dynamic Topology**: Every corridor, junction, and wall is alive. Paths slide, rotate, expand, collapse, and reform based on the threat’s behavior.
- **Active Herding**: The labyrinth doesn’t wait to be attacked. It predicts movement, seals escape routes, and continuously funnels the Minotaur deeper.
- **The Kill Zone**: Once the threat reaches the center, the walls close completely. Isolation, process termination, cryptographic shredding, and behavioral erasure occur.
- **Self-Evolution**: After every engagement the system learns. Successful strategies become permanent. Failed approaches are discarded. The labyrinth grows smarter with every Minotaur it kills.
