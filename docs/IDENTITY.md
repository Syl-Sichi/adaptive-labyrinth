# Adaptive Labyrinth — Name & Identity

## Official Name
**Adaptive Labyrinth**

## Tagline
**A living maze for digital monsters.**

## Visual Style
- Dark, deep void backgrounds
- Glowing geometric walls in cool cyan / electric blue that shift and reconfigure with purpose
- The Minotaur represented as a sharp, aggressive red/orange entity
- The Kill Zone at the center pulses with intense white/crimson energy when activated
- Clean, high-contrast aesthetic blending mythology and cyberpunk
- Motion is fluid, deliberate, and predatory — never random or chaotic

## Origin Story

In the old myths, Daedalus built a labyrinth to contain a monster.  
We built one to hunt them.

Static walls failed. Signatures became obsolete. Threats learned to move through the cracks between traditional defenses. So the Labyrinth was redesigned — not as a prison, but as a predator. Its corridors are alive. Its walls rearrange themselves with purpose. Every path exists to drive the Minotaur toward the center, where no escape remains and destruction is absolute.

The Adaptive Labyrinth does not wait to be breached.  
It hunts.
